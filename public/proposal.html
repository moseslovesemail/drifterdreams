THE OPPORTUNITY

One Dog Club.
Multiple Drifters.

Drifter Dog Club began with a simple idea: give guests an easy way to connect with dogs, dog owners and dog-friendly social experiences during their stay.

The existing Christchurch concept can now become a live multi-property platform. Each Drifter can have its own dogs, activity and property information while remaining part of one shared system.

Dog Club can link directly from Drifter’s existing guest web app, complementing the guest experience already in place rather than replacing it.
